Glean Message Enhance

Generates Subject Line, Messaging, Reference Script, and WhatsApp Message via Glean for a batch of contacts. Use rows already in the tracker, or upload a fresh CSV.

Once staged, each row has an editable "Additional Context" column — leave it blank for the usual generic prompt, or fill it in (e.g. "part of the IDC campaign, use that as the hook") to have it appended to that row's email, WhatsApp, and cold call prompts.

Option 1 — ZoomInfo Contact Only

Scrapes your filtered ZoomInfo People Search results via the internal API (no clicking required). Populates Patch, Company Name, Contact Name, Title / Role, LinkedIn Profile, ZoomInfo Phone, ZoomInfo Email.

Click Start, then log in to ZoomInfo and set up your filters in the browser window that opens. Use Send (blank + Send = ENTER) to answer any prompts shown in the log.

Option 2 — ZoomInfo LinkedIn Scrap

Same ZoomInfo scrape as Option 1, then enriches every contact via LinkedIn Sales Navigator (About, Experience Description, Latest Post, Tenure, LinkedIn Phone, LinkedIn Email). Requires logging into both ZoomInfo and Sales Navigator.

Idle {% if linkedin_has_staging %} Preview / Commit results → {% endif %}

Click Start, log in to ZoomInfo first, then Sales Navigator when prompted. A ZoomInfo-only checkpoint CSV is saved automatically before the LinkedIn phase begins.
